Prompt 12:
Why is there a gap between my School, Degree, Graduation, etc in the About section?

Response 12:

Yes — the gap is coming from .bio-meta being a grid.
Your .bio-grid has two columns, and the right column (.bio-description) is much taller. CSS Grid makes the left .bio-meta stretch to the same height as the right column. Since .bio-meta is itself a grid, its rows are being distributed across all that extra height.
Change:
.bio-meta {
    display: grid;
    font-size: 0.92rem;
}
to:
.bio-meta {
    display: flex;
    flex-direction: column;
    gap: 18px;
    justify-content: flex-start;

    font-size: 0.92rem;
}
