At CMU, I have spent the last three years studying statistics, machine learning, and related areas of data and computing. I have been an athlete my entire life, and that experience has led me to explore different areas of sports through data and technology.

My projects have involved building the pieces around a model, not just the model itself. I have worked with raw sports data, designed databases to organize it, built pipelines to prepare it for analysis, and developed the models and optimzation methods that use it. I have also built web-based and Shiny applications to make the work accessible outside of analysis. Because I started in Computer Science before moving into Statistics and Machine Learning, I am interested in applying those skills across both software engineering and data focused roles.
